## Executive summary

All high- and medium-priority items from the original review are now landed or
prototyped. The repo gained CI enforcement (`lint-test` on ubuntu+windows plus a
scaffold smoke job), a machine-readable `doctor` gate, working cross-platform
persistence in the generated app (preferences + dark palette + a real list
page), an opt-in Vitest scaffold, scaffold-failure rollback, and several small
correctness fixes (`detectPm` fallback, pure `applyRunner`, content-driven
token replacement). Verification state at close-out: 70/70 unit tests,
`tsc --noEmit` clean, `bun run build` clean.
